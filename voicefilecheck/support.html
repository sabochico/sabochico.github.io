How do I check a recording?

Import from Files, open the recording and enter the client’s requirements. Read the results, listen to the audio and confirm any manual requirements.

Will the original file change?

The app imports a separate copy and creates a new file for renaming or processing. Exported audio is analyzed again before sharing.

Which files can I export?

Mono/stereo WAV at 16/24-bit, or M4A AAC, using 44.1/48 kHz for M4A and 44.1/48/96 kHz for WAV. MP3 import is supported by iOS; MP3 export is unavailable.

Is LUFS or noise-floor certification available?

Pro measures integrated LUFS, maximum momentary and short-term loudness, true peak and loudness range using libebur128. Mono/stereo is supported; short clips may not support every measurement. Quiet-window estimates are affected by content and are not isolated room noise. Sample-peak normalization does not perform true-peak limiting.

How do I restore Pro?

Open Settings → Restore Purchases using the Apple Account that purchased Pro. Cancellation and pending purchases do not unlock Pro. Verified StoreKit entitlements provide access offline when available on the device.

How do I save a backup of audio?

Open a recording, choose Share recording and select Save to Files. Keep important files outside the app before deleting the app.
