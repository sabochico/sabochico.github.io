Privacy policy

Updated 27 November 2026. This policy describes the audio submission application developed by Joshua Sho Thompson.

Audio and library data

Recordings, projects, client details, requirement and naming templates, loudness measurements and reports are processed and stored locally on your device. We operate no audio-upload server, account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or AI processing service.

Purchases

Apple handles payments through StoreKit under Apple’s own policies. The app receives verified purchase entitlement information to enable Pro. We do not receive your card details.

Sharing and backups

You choose whether to share or export a file using system services. The destination you select receives that material. Device or iCloud backups may include app data according to your Apple settings.

Support

If you email support, we receive your message, sender details and attachments you choose to include. These are used to respond to your request. Send only the information needed to resolve the issue. Confidential audition audio is not required.

Deletion

Delete recordings, projects and templates in the app. Removing the app removes its local library subject to device backup behavior. Exported and shared copies are managed separately by their destinations. Contact support to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable retention obligations.

This website

The site contains no analytics scripts or advertising. Its hosting provider may process standard request information necessary to deliver the website. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard request information under its own privacy policy.
