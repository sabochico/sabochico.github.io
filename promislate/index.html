Help & support

Create a case with a company and issue title. Open the case to add conversations, promises and evidence. Use Edit to set a follow-up date and optional reminder. Mark the case resolved when you are finished.

Evidence and exports

Attach a photo, screenshot or PDF up to 20 MB. Export a PDF for a readable summary. Export a .promislate case file to include original attachments. Settings → Export full backup saves every case. Keep backups private.

Restore a backup

Settings → Import backup or case. Other cases remain. A matching case ID is replaced by the imported version. Imported reminders are off until you review and enable them. Backup files have a 100 MB limit.

Reminders

Enable notifications in iPhone Settings. Focus and notification preferences can delay or silence alerts. Dates are personal follow-up reminders, not legal deadlines.

Purchases

Five active cases are free. Plus is a one-time Apple in-app purchase for unlimited active cases. Use Restore purchases with the Apple Account that bought Plus. Existing records and basic exports remain accessible.

Keeping your data

Back up regularly. Deleting the app may remove local records. App lock does not protect exported copies. This app does not guarantee refunds, contact businesses, verify evidence or provide legal advice.

For help, email aweandco@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version and a description of the problem. Only attach case records or evidence if you choose to share them.

Privacy policy

Prepared 21 November 2026.

Promislate stores cases, notes, dates and attachments in private app storage on your device. It has no mandatory account, advertising SDK or developer-operated case server. Case contents are not sent to the developer by the app.

Apple device backups may contain app records according to your settings. Selecting an export or backup destination shares those records with that destination. Copying a summary places its contents on your system clipboard.

Apple processes purchases and purchase restoration. The app uses verified purchase information to unlock Plus. Apple may provide store analytics and diagnostic information according to your Apple settings.

Optional local notifications use a generic message without company details. Optional app lock uses iOS authentication. The app does not receive your biometric data. The photo and file pickers grant access to the items you select; Promislate does not read your inbox, calls or bank accounts.

You can delete cases in the app. Keep exports and backups secure, and remove any copies you no longer need from their respective destinations. Uninstalling the app does not delete exports stored elsewhere.

If you choose to contact support, share only the information needed to explain your problem. Do not send sensitive evidence unnecessarily.
